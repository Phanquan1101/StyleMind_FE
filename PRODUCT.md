# Product

## Register

product

## Users

StyleMind store administrators use this dashboard while managing the Vietnamese fashion shop. They scan sales and catalog summaries, review customer requests, and monitor the AI outfit experience as part of day-to-day operations.

## Product Purpose

StyleMind helps customers discover and manage outfits, while the admin area keeps the shop's catalog, orders, and customer support workflows running. The dashboard should make the next operational action clear and help administrators reach the relevant queue quickly.

## Brand Personality

Refined, helpful, inclusive. Keep language clear and respectful, with a calm tone that supports administrators without making routine work feel urgent or punitive.

## Anti-references

- Generic dashboard templates with repeated, interchangeable KPI cards.
- Large decorative serif headings that compete with operational information.
- Dense layouts that hide pending work or make status depend on color alone.

## Design Principles

- Put actionable work where administrators can find it quickly.
- Show what a number covers so it can be interpreted in context.
- Keep operational language concise, calm, and inclusive.
- Preserve familiar navigation between the admin tools and the customer storefront.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, clear labels, and status cues that do not rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep interactive targets at least 44 by 44 pixels where practical.
